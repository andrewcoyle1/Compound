# Motion, press feedback and Undo: UX review (16 Nov 2026)

This review covers the work on branch `claude/charles-ux-animation-ybakfv`, which brings the
rules of the `charles-ux-animation` skill (LemonFace0309/charles-skills at `9b4a8a3`) into the
Flutter app: the motion tokens, press feedback, icon swaps, delayed spinners, an error shake,
reduced motion, and Undo in place of confirmation for single deletes.

**What was checked:**
- Every change ran in widget tests on the Linux test host: light theme, 1× text, reduced motion
  both on and off where it matters.
- Nothing ran on a device or simulator. Dark mode, large text, VoiceOver and the feel of the
  timings still need checking by hand (the list is at the end).

**HIG claims are unverified.** The `apple-hig` skill (`~/.claude/skills/apple-hig`) is not
installed in the session that did this work, so no claim below was checked against Apple's live
Human Interface Guidelines. Run the pages listed under "To check against the HIG" before merging.

## What changed

| Area | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Tokens | `quick` 200, `standard` 350, `emphasis` 500, `progress` 1000 ms; stock curves; no exit timing | Adds `press` (120 ms) and `move` (350 ms); `standard` drops to 300 ms; a strong ease-out and ease-in-out; exits a quarter shorter, on `exitCurve` |
| Press | InkWell highlight only | Controls up to 200 pt wide shrink to 0.96 (`AppPressScale`); rows and cards keep the highlight |
| Icon swaps | Cut | Scale 0.3 → 1 with a fade, in place (`AppIconSwap`, `AppDoneMark.toggle`) |
| Spinners in buttons | At once | After 300 ms, held for at least 500 ms (`AppBusySwap`) |
| Spinner under Reduce Motion | Held still | Keeps turning |
| Dialogs under Reduce Motion | Animated | Appear and go without a transition |
| Failed username save | Dialog only | The field shakes once (`AppShake`), with the error haptic |
| Single deletes | Confirm first, or no way back at all for swipes | At once, with a six-second Undo snack bar; written when the Undo goes |

## Findings

### 1. A delete waiting on an Undo is lost if the app is killed

**Severity: polish (by design).**

- **Where:** `app/lib/undo/pending_deletes.dart`.
- **What happens:** a delete is written when its Undo goes, when the app goes to the background
  (`AppLifecycleListener.onHide`), and before sign-out (`FlushingAuthRepository`). If the app is
  killed in the six seconds, it is never written and the item survives.
- **Fix:** none needed. Losing the delete is the safe way to fail, and is documented in the class.

### 2. Screens that are not on view can show a pending item for up to six seconds

**Severity: polish.**

- **Where:** Today's nutrition totals and the timeline, during a meal or food Undo.
- **What happens:** only the lists the user deletes from hide a pending item. A screen elsewhere
  catches up when the write lands.
- **Fix:** none planned. Sessions keep their confirmation partly for this reason (twelve readers).

### 3. Full-width buttons do not scale when pressed

**Severity: owner decision.**

- **Where:** `AppPressScale.maxWidth` (200 pt).
- **What happens:** a regular `AppCtaButton` is full width, so it keeps the highlight only, as rows
  and cards do.
- **Fix, if wanted:** a smaller scale (0.98) for wide controls instead of none.

### 4. Custom curves on iOS

**Severity: unverified against the HIG.**

- **Where:** `AppMotion`.
- **What happens:** the curves are the skill's, not UIKit's springs. The platform's own push, sheet
  and dialog transitions are untouched (design-language.md).
- **To check:** whether in-place motion should follow iOS spring timing more closely.
